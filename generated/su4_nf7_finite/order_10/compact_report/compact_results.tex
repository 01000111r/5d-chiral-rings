The flavour symmetry is $\mathrm{SU}(7)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{\beta}$.
The expansion is truncated at $O(t^{11})$.  We define
$[a_1,\ldots,a_{6}]:=[a_1,\ldots,a_{6}]_{A6}$.
Dimension evaluation is the ring homomorphism
$\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(7)}:R(\mathrm{SU}(7))\to\mathbb{Z}$, with
$H_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,\beta)=\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(7)}H(t,\beta;\mathrm{SU}(7))$
and $H(t)=H_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,1)$. Likewise,
$\operatorname{PL}_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,\beta)=\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(7)}
\operatorname{PL}[H(t,\beta;\mathrm{SU}(7))]$ and
$\operatorname{PL}[H(t)]=\operatorname{PL}_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,1)$;
the latter agrees with the independently stored scalar result.
\section{Finite-coupling source specialization}
Equation (5.52) gives
\[\mathrm{HWG}^{\mathrm{finite}}_{N_c,N_f}=\PE\!\left[t^2+(\mu_{N_c}\beta+\mu_{N_f-N_c}\beta^{-1})t^{N_c}+\sum_{j=1}^{N_c}\mu_j\mu_{N_f-j}t^{2j}-\mu_{N_c}\mu_{N_f-N_c}t^{2N_c}\right].\]
For $N_c=4,N_f=7$, the unsimplified exponent is
\[t^2+(\mu_4\beta+\mu_3\beta^{-1})t^4+\mu_1\mu_6t^2+\mu_2\mu_5t^4+\mu_3\mu_4t^6+\mu_4\mu_3t^8-\mu_4\mu_3t^8.\]
Thus $+\mu_4\mu_3t^8-\mu_4\mu_3t^8=0$ exactly.
