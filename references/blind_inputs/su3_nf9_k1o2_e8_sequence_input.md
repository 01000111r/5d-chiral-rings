# Blind benchmark input: SU(3) with 9 flavours at |k| = 1/2

## Source

G. Ferlito, A. Hanany, N. Mekareeya and G. Zafrir,
"3d Coulomb branch and 5d Higgs branch at infinite coupling",
arXiv:1712.06604, section 2.2, equation (2.4).

## General source formula

For SU(n)_{±1/2} with N_f = 2n+3 at infinite coupling,

\[
\operatorname{HWG}
=
\operatorname{PE}\left[
\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}\mu_{2i}t^{2i}
+t^4
+\mu_{2n+4}(t^n+t^{n+2})
\right].
\]

The enhanced non-abelian symmetry for n>2 is SO(4n+8).

## Fixed-rank benchmark input

Set n=3. Then:

- gauge theory: SU(3) with N_f=9;
- Chern-Simons levels: the sign pair ±1/2;
- coupling: infinite;
- enhanced symmetry: SO(20);
- Cartan type: D10;
- grading variable: t;
- highest-weight fugacities: mu_1,...,mu_10;
- there are no external abelian fugacities.

The specialized HWG is

\[
\operatorname{PE}\left[
\mu_2t^2
+\mu_{10}t^3
+(1+\mu_4)t^4
+\mu_{10}t^5
+\mu_6t^6
+\mu_8t^8
\right].
\]

## Withheld information

The Hilbert-series and plethystic-logarithm outputs printed later in the
paper are intentionally excluded from this file. They must not be used
while producing the benchmark calculation.
