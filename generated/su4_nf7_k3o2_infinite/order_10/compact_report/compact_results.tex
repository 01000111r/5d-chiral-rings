The flavour symmetry is $\mathrm{SU}(8)\times\mathrm{U}(1)_{q}$.
The expansion is truncated at $O(t^{11})$.  We define
$[a_1,\ldots,a_{7}]:=[a_1,\ldots,a_{7}]_{A7}$.
Dimension evaluation is the ring homomorphism
$\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(8)}:R(\mathrm{SU}(8))\to\mathbb{Z}$, with
$H_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,q)=\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(8)}H(t,q;\mathrm{SU}(8))$
and $H(t)=H_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,1)$. Likewise,
$\operatorname{PL}_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,q)=\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(8)}
\operatorname{PL}[H(t,q;\mathrm{SU}(8))]$ and
$\operatorname{PL}[H(t)]=\operatorname{PL}_{\mathrm{dim}}(t,1)$;
the latter agrees with the independently stored scalar result.
