# Hanany negative-CS convention

## Approved source

G. Ferlito, A. Hanany, N. Mekareeya and G. Zafrir, “3d Coulomb branch and
5d Higgs branch at infinite coupling”, JHEP 07 (2018) 061,
arXiv:1712.06604. This note and the campaign specification are the approved
convention source; committing the paper PDF is not required.

## Statements in the paper

Section 2.3 (printed page 14) states that one-instanton charges depend on the
CS-level sign and chooses the minus sign. The I=+1 currents occur in the
specified flavour representations with baryon charges -n+1/2 and n+1/2;
the anti-instanton supplies the complex-conjugate representations. Section
6.3 (printed page 21) again chooses the minus sign: the I=+1 state is in the
(n+1)-index antisymmetric representation of SU(2n+1), while the
anti-instanton is in the conjugate n-index antisymmetric representation.

## Family representation evidence

The relevant family assignments are in sections 7.1 (SU(n)_{±2}+2nF), 8.1
(SU(n)_{±1}+2nF), 9.3 (SU(n)_0+2nF), 10.1
(SU(n)_{±5/2}+(2n-1)F), 11.1 (SU(n)_{±3/2}+(2n-1)F), and 12.1
(SU(n)_{±1/2}+(2n-1)F). These sections supply family-specific
representation information; where a section does not attach a sign to a
representation, the signed assignment is project-derived, not quoted as a
direct literature fact.

## Project derivation and orientation

The project uses microscopic baryon number B, normalized by B(Q)=1, and
integer instanton number I. For SU(N)_k the exact zero-mode convention is
B(E_+)=N-k and B(E_-)=-N-k, with I(E_±)=+1. All five nonzero cases select
the E_- representation at the configured negative signed k. Exact stored
branching identifies that representation and its conjugate; a finite
beta-graded baryon or antibaryon supplies the second anchor, with B=3B_beta.
These are project derivations and orientation choices extending the paper’s
negative-CS convention uniformly across the six cases. In particular, they
are not claims that every final raw-to-(B,I) matrix is printed in the paper.

Operator conjugation at fixed k sends (rho,B,I) to (conjugate(rho),-B,-I).
Reversing k is different: it selects the appropriate conjugate I=+1
representation and recomputes its microscopic B at the new signed level.
The legacy instanton-current-neutral shifted basis is noncanonical and must
never be called B.
