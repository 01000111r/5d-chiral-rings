At infinite coupling the enhanced symmetry is $\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2}$.
The expansion is truncated at $O(t^{11})$.  We define
$[a_1,\ldots,a_{5};b;c]:=[a_1,\ldots,a_{5}]_{A5}\otimes[b]_{A1^{(1)}}\otimes[c]_{A1^{(2)}}$.
Dimension evaluation is the ring homomorphism
$\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2}}:R(\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2})\to\mathbb{Z}$, with
$H(t)=\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2}}H(t;\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2})$ and
$\operatorname{PL}[H(t)]=\dim_{\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2}}
\operatorname{PL}[H(t;\mathrm{SU}(6)\times\mathrm{SU(2)_1}\times\mathrm{SU(2)_2})]$;
the latter agrees with the independently stored scalar result.
