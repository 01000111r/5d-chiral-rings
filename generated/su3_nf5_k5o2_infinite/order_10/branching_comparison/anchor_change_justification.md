The old degree-three parent `[0,0,0,1,0]` at q=+1 has `[0,1,0,0]` at x=+1, so it is the conjugate antibaryon and cannot match finite `beta [0,0,1,0]`. The finite beta power is one, hence B=3 from B(Q)=1 for the three-quark SU(3) baryon and I=0. The degree-two current anchor `[0,1,0,0]` at `(-4,0)` remains fixed by the adjoint restriction and signed-CS evidence `-3-(-5/2)=-1/2`, I=1.
